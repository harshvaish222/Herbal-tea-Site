A few helpful details

Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

2 to 5 working days depending on your pincode. Use the delivery check to see yours.

Can I return tea?

Unopened packs can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Opened tea cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

How should I store my tea?

In an airtight container, away from light, heat and strong smells. It tastes best within 6 months of opening.

Is Chamomile & Tulsi caffeine-free?

Yes, it is a caffeine-free herbal blend.

Do you ship outside India?

Not yet. We currently deliver within India only.

Can I send tea as a gift?

Yes. The Tea Lover's Sampler comes in a wooden gift box with a brewing guide.
